Securing Your Smart Home Network

Your home Wi-Fi network connects all your smart devices, including cameras, smart locks, and voice assistants. If your network is not properly secured, hackers may try to access it and control connected devices.

Physical Safety and Smart Home Devices

Smart home technology can also help protect people and property from physical dangers. Smart smoke detectors, carbon monoxide alarms, motion sensors, and security cameras can help homeowners identify potential hazards.

Protecting Smart Devices

Smart devices make everyday activities easier, but they can also introduce cybersecurity risks. Devices such as smart locks, security cameras, and video doorbells may contain vulnerabilities if they are not properly maintained.

Monitoring and Responding to Threats

Protecting your smart home does not stop after installing security devices. You should regularly monitor your connected devices for unusual activity, such as unexpected logins or unfamiliar device connections.

Security Checklist

  • Passwords

    Remind users to change default passwords and use unique ones.

  • Wi-Fi

    Remind users to secure their network with WPA2 or WPA3.

  • Authentication

    Encourage two-factor authentication.

  • Updates

    Remind users to install firmware update

  • Monitoring

    Encourage users to check for unusual device activity.
